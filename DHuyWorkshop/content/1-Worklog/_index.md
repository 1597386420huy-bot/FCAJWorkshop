Over 12 weeks, the team focused on AWS infrastructure for a phone-store web application. The work started with foundational knowledge of cloud, networking and access management, moved on to planning and preparing the system, and then to deployment, testing and monitoring. The phases are organized as follows, with the whole team working together to build the infrastructure for the phone-store application.

The activities of the 12 weeks are listed below.

- Week 1: [Get familiar with AWS and plan the deployment architecture](/1-Worklog/1.1-Week1/)
- Week 2: [Learn and configure AWS IAM](/1-Worklog/1.2-Week2/)
- Week 3: [Design the Amazon VPC and network access rules](/1-Worklog/1.3-Week3/)
- Week 4: [Prepare the Amazon EC2 application server](/1-Worklog/1.4-Week4/)
- Week 5: [Configure Amazon RDS for MySQL](/1-Worklog/1.5-Week5/)
- Week 6: [Set up Amazon S3 for frontend assets and images](/1-Worklog/1.6-Week6/)
- Week 7: [Deliver frontend assets with Amazon CloudFront](/1-Worklog/1.7-Week7/)
- Week 8: [Deploy Spring Boot to EC2](/1-Worklog/1.8-Week8/)
- Week 9: [Integrate the frontend, backend and database connection](/1-Worklog/1.9-Week9/)
- Week 10: [Finalize image storage and delivery](/1-Worklog/1.10-Week10/)
- Week 11: [Monitor services with Amazon CloudWatch](/1-Worklog/1.11-Week11/)
- Week 12: [End-to-end testing and workshop documentation](/1-Worklog/1.12-Week12/)
