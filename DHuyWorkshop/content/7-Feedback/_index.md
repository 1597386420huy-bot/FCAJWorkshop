### General Evaluation

- **Working environment:** friendly, open and comfortable; one suggestion is to organize more social or team-building activities.
- **Mentor and team management:** appreciated the guidance, the encouragement to ask questions and the administrative support.
- **Relevance to my major:** the assigned work connects foundational knowledge from school with new, real-world topics.
- **Learning opportunities:** project tooling, teamwork, professional communication and career orientation were emphasized.
- **Culture and teamwork:** described mutual respect and colleagues supporting one another.
- **Internship policies and benefits:** an allowance, some schedule flexibility and access to internal training courses.
